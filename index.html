Fees & What's Included

2027 Team Fee: $1,500 per athlete. A payment is due at the first practice in January, with the full balance due by the end of February unless another arrangement is approved.

Included

  • Tournament entry fees
  • Provincial entry fee
  • National entry fee where applicable
  • Uniform and hoodie
  • Coach expenses and honorarium
  • Administration

Family Responsibilities

  • Transportation
  • Accommodation
  • Meals
  • Personal expenses

Payment may be made by e-transfer, cash, or postdated cheque.

Club Standards

Policies & Expectations

Athlete Code of Conduct

Respect teammates, coaches, officials, opponents, parents and volunteers; arrive prepared and on time; work hard; maintain positive body language; accept feedback; support teammates; follow rules; care for equipment and facilities; demonstrate sportsmanship; and represent Big Bear positively.

Coach Code of Conduct

Coaches put athlete safety, development and well-being first; provide a positive environment; arrive prepared; communicate appropriately; maintain boundaries and confidentiality; follow club and MVA requirements; report safety concerns; and disclose conflicts of interest.

Parent / Guardian Code of Conduct

Parents support athletes and the team, respect coaching decisions, avoid coaching from the sidelines, use the 24-hour rule for non-emergency concerns, communicate privately and respectfully, follow tournament rules, and represent Big Bear positively.

24-Hour Rule & Complaint Resolution

For non-emergency concerns, parents wait 24 hours, then contact the coach privately. If unresolved, the coach and parent may involve the Club Director. Safety, harassment, discrimination, abuse, bullying, threats, or serious misconduct should be reported immediately.

Conflict of Interest

Actual, potential or perceived conflicts should be disclosed to the Club Director. The club may require recusal, another decision-maker, additional information, documentation, or other reasonable steps to protect fairness and transparency.

Payment & Refund Policy

Refunds may be considered before the season begins, less non-refundable committed costs. After participation begins, refunds are generally not provided, although exceptional circumstances such as significant injury or illness may be reviewed. Refund requests should be made in writing.

Social Media

Use privacy settings responsibly, be aware of who can access posts, avoid derogatory comments about athletes, coaches or the club, and do not post inappropriate material associated with Big Bear Volleyball.
